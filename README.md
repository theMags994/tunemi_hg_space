# TuneMi 🎙️ — Hear Yourself, Not a Stranger

**[Try the full experience at tunemi.space →](https://tunemi.space/)**

TuneMi is a free accent refinement tool built on one idea: record yourself speaking, upload a clip, or paste a Vocaroo link — and hear the same words played back **in your own voice**, with a refined accent. Not a generic AI narrator. Not a stock voice actor. You.

Most accent tools ask you to trust them. TuneMi asks you to trust your own ears instead.

## How it works

1. **Record** directly in your browser, **upload** an audio file, or **paste a Vocaroo link**
2. TuneMi processes your speech and refines the accent while preserving your voice
3. **Listen back**, compare, and judge the result yourself — no black-box AI voice standing in for you
4. **Download** the result or **share it via a unique link** — no account required

Supported formats: `MP3`, `WAV`, `M4A`, `OGG`, `FLAC`

## Why it's different

- 🎯 **Self-auditing by design** — because the output is still recognizably *you*, you can judge in real time whether it actually worked, not take a rating's word for it
- 🆓 **Completely free** — ad-supported, no subscription, no paywall on the core experience
- 🔓 **No account needed** — no gate between "I'm curious" and "I heard the result"
- 🔗 **Shareable & saveable** — download your refined audio or send a link to a coach, friend, or hiring manager

## 🎤 A note on microphone access

This Space uses your browser's microphone to record audio directly. If you're viewing this embedded on another website, the host page must grant microphone permission via `allow="microphone"` on the iframe for live recording to work. If it doesn't, use the **upload** or **Vocaroo link** option instead — no permissions required.

## Embed this Space

```html
<iframe
  src="https://<space-subdomain>.hf.space"
  frameborder="0"
  width="850"
  height="450"
  allow="microphone">
</iframe>
```
